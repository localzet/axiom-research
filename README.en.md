# axiom-research v0.2.0

DOCX-first bilingual research repository. The primary research pack is supplied as two complete anthologies: English and
Russian. TeX/BibTeX sources are intentionally absent from v0.2.

- `docx/Axiom-Research-Pack-v0.2.0-EN.docx`
- `docx/Axiom-Research-Pack-v0.2.0-RU.docx`
- `roadmap/Axiom-Roadmap-v0.2.0-EN.docx`
- `roadmap/Axiom-Roadmap-v0.2.0-RU.docx`

`PRIOR_ART.md` and `NOVELTY_BOUNDARY.md` document the research boundary; Russian variants are provided alongside them.
